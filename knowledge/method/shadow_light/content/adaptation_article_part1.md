# Adaptation After Relocation — Core Concept

## Main principle

Adaptation is influenced not only by external circumstances but also by how a woman experiences those circumstances internally.

## Examples of external factors

- language barrier;
- new culture;
- unfamiliar environment;
- changed life circumstances.

## Internal dimension

The same situation can be experienced differently depending on internal dialogue and beliefs.

Key principle:

"Обстоятельства создают ситуацию. А мысли определяют, как мы её проживаем."
